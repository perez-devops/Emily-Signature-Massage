# Imagery & Photography — Boutique Wellness / Holistic Massage

## Lighting

- Natural, soft, directional — window light, golden hour, overcast daylight
- Never: flat studio lighting, ring-light glow, harsh clinical fluorescents, or heavy blue-toned "medical"
  color grading

## Subject Matter (recurring motifs)

- **Hands**: kneading, pouring warm oil, arranging dried botanicals, holding a warm stone — hands are the
  brand's signature motif, more than faces
- **Texture close-ups**: linen weave, raw wood grain, unglazed ceramic, dried herbs/flowers, stone surfaces
- **Environment**: treatment room with natural materials visible, window light on a massage table, a garden
  or windowsill with plants — shows the space, not just the service
- **Product**: oil bottles, candles in matte/unglazed vessels — shot with visible texture and shadow, not
  glossy product-catalog lighting

## Color Grading

- Warm, true-to-life skin tones — no bleached/blue "clinical" grade, no oversaturated "Instagram filter" grade
- Shadows lean toward Bark (`#2E2620`) rather than pure black
- Highlights lean toward Warm Paper (`#F4EEE4`) rather than blown-out white

## Composition

- Editorial, observational framing — as if a photo essay about a real place, not a posed stock shoot
- Negative space is welcome (aligns with generous type layout) — don't fill every frame
- Crop tight on texture/hands sometimes; pull back to show full environment other times — vary rhythm across
  a social grid or gallery rather than repeating the same framing

## Explicit Avoid List (screen every generated/sourced image against this)

- Stacked stones + bamboo + water droplets (generic spa stock cliché)
- Lotus flower icons/graphics
- Teal, purple, or lavender color grading
- Glossy, heavily retouched skin close-ups
- Smiling posed portraits looking at camera (reads corporate marketing, not documentary/editorial)
- Any imagery that could be mistaken for a medical/clinical brand (white coats, clinical equipment, sterile
  white rooms)
- Neon or bright gradient overlays
- Generic "wellness app" iconography (meditating figure silhouettes, chakra symbols, infinity/lotus line icons)

## AI Generation Prompt Language

When generating imagery with an image model, favor language like:

> "natural window light, warm tones, linen and raw wood textures, hands pouring oil, documentary/editorial
> photography style, shallow depth of field, no text"

Negative-prompt or explicitly avoid:

> "studio lighting, teal color grade, stock photo, lotus flower, stacked stones, glossy retouched skin,
> neon, gradient background, corporate portrait"
