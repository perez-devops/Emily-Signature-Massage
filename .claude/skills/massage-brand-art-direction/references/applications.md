# Application Notes — Boutique Wellness / Holistic Massage

How the direction (`../SKILL.md`, `palette.md`, `typography.md`, `imagery.md`) translates per touchpoint.

## Logo / Mark

- A simple botanical linework mark (single leaf/sprig, or an abstracted hand-forms-a-shape mark) or a
  restrained wordmark set in the display serif (Fraunces or equivalent)
- Single color (Bark or Sage) — no gradients, no glossy 3D, no drop shadows
- Must work as a one-color stamp (e.g. wax-seal-style embossed on packaging) — test the mark at small sizes
  in flat single color before finalizing

## Website

- Warm Paper background throughout — avoid pure white sections
- Generous whitespace, large display-serif headlines, editorial photography full-bleed in hero sections
- Booking CTA uses Sage as button color, Bark text on it (check contrast)
- Avoid sticky/aggressive UI patterns (popups, countdown timers) — the site should feel unhurried, matching
  the brand's pacing

## Social (Instagram/Facebook grid)

- Vary composition per `imagery.md` rhythm — don't repeat the same crop/filter every post
- Text overlays (when used) in Bark on Warm Paper or Sand card, display serif for the headline, sans for
  supporting text
- Grid-level consistency: same color grade and margin/whitespace treatment across posts, not the same
  template repeated

## Signage / Environmental

- Natural materials preferred for physical signage (wood, brushed metal, matte finishes) over glossy acrylic
- If printed, use uncoated/matte stock — glossy finish undercuts the tactile, grounded feel

## Packaging (oils, candles, retail)

- Matte or unglazed label stock, Bark ink on Sand or Warm Paper label
- Terracotta as trim/seasonal accent color on packaging specifically (this is where Terracotta gets its
  most visible use)
- Avoid glossy varnish, foil stamping, or high-shine finishes

## Print (business cards, gift certificates, price lists)

- Uncoated/matte card stock, letterpress or blind-emboss feel if budget allows for the logo mark
- Sand or Warm Paper stock color, Bark ink
- Gift certificates may use Terracotta as the accent (seasonal/gifting association)

## Uniforms / Staff Apparel

- Neutral tones (Warm Paper, Sand, Stone) as base garment colors
- Sage as the one branded accent (e.g. embroidered logo mark), not a full-color branded uniform
- Avoid loud branding on apparel — the therapist's presence should feel calm, not like a branded polo shirt
