How it works here

No two sessions are the same, because no two bodies are.

Before any table time, we talk — about where you're carrying tension, what's changed since your last session, what you want to feel like when you leave. The work follows that conversation, not a fixed routine.

That means your first visit runs a few minutes longer than a typical massage. If you're looking for something you can walk straight into with zero conversation, this isn't that — it's built for people who want the work actually aimed at what's wrong.
